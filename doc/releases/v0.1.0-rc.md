## Acceptance and changes

The initial candidate had local pure/race, generated/API, cross-build, bounded
fuzz/stress and benchmark evidence. Hosted checkout CI starts on publication.
The English/header update must retain declaration documentation, generated
consistency, public consumer behavior and the no-persistence boundary. The
installation gate requires two independent cold caches, GOWORK=off, no replace,
authenticated module downloads, exact graphs and pure/race consumer execution.

See [version policy](../version-install-policy.md), [checkout checks](../release-checks.md)
and [current evidence](../../spec/progress.json). Historical pre-publication 404s
are retained as failed attempts, not reclassified as successful installation.
